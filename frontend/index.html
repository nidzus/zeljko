Who is this chart for?

Where were they born?

    Right-click a spot in Google Maps and copy the numbers. Any format works — 44.7866, 20.4489 or 44°47'11"N 20°26'56"E.

    When?

    Local clock time at the birthplace, as it was recorded. We handle the timezone rules that were in force that day.

    How well is that time known?

    This is not a formality. It decides what the app is allowed to tell you — and it will refuse to answer beyond what your birth time can actually support.

    A few dated life events

    Five to ten is plenty. Job changes, moves, marriages, losses, the years things turned. These give the advisor a history to reason from, and later they are what a birth-time rectification is tested against.
